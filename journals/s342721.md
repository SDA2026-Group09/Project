After divide the workload, I did some search about the Software Architecture section.

I started by diving into the Processing 4 repository to explore its main modules (app, core, and java). Using the C4 model, I wrote the code to generate our Context and Container diagrams with PlantUML. During this process, I identified that the system relies on a Microkernel (plug-in) architecture and analyzed how its core is separated from the UI, checking its alignment with Clean Architecture.

After that, I zoomed into the IDE for the Component level (Level 3). I evaluated the internal structure and discovered that the Editor acts as a "God Class", which clearly violates the Single Responsibility Principle (SRP) by handling too many tasks at once.

I have also documented key architectural characteristics like portability, extensibility, and coupling/cohesion. I have a very clear understanding of our architecture now, and the text and diagrams are ready to be merged into our final report.
